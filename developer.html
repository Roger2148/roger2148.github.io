Japanese Speaking Teacher

Self-hosted speaking practice over LAN or Tailscale · alpha

You talk in Japanese or English, the app transcribes it, a local language model answers as a tutor with corrections, and the reply is spoken back. Topic starters, adjustable correction intensity, saved history, works from a phone on the home network. No cloud, no API keys.
